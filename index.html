How To Stay Safe Online

In today's digital age staying safe online is more important than ever. With technology evolving rapidly it is important to unserstand and learn about the ways scammers and hackers operate online and how to protect yourself. This guide will walk you through things to look out for and even test your knowledge.

Common Online Threats and Scams

  • Phishing Emails and Texts

    • According to New York States office of information technology and the FBI over 6,000 reports on internet fraud were filed in 2024 from people over the age of 60. This demographic is particularly vulnerable and often targeted by scammers.

  • Fake Websites and Medicare Scams

    • Fake websites and Medicare scams are another common way scammers trick people into hading over sensitive information that could cause lasting detrimental effects on a persons life. Often times medicare scams and fake websites appear convincng and legitimate to unsuspecting users.

  • Tech Support Pop-ups

    • Tech support pop-ups are deceptive alerts that claim your computer has a problem and prompt you to call a fake support number. Scammers use these pop-ups to gain remote access to your device and steal personal information. Pop-ups can be very convincing and can often time scare users into clicking the pop-up and accidentally downloading malware into the user's device.
