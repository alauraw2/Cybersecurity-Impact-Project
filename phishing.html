Phishing Emails and Texts

Phishing emails and texts are fraudulent messages designed to trick you into revealing personal information, such as passwords, credit card numbers, or Social Security numbers. These messages often appear to come from legitimate sources, like banks, government agencies, or well-known companies.

according to R.O.S.E Advocacy in 2024, the average estimated financial loss from phishing attacks was estimated to be around $38,500 per incident for people over 60.

To protect yourself from phishing attacks, always verify the sender's email address or phone number, avoid clicking on suspicious links, and never provide sensitive information in response to unsolicited messages.

How to Recognize Phishing Attempts

  • Check for spelling and grammar errors in the message.

    • example: Amaz0n.com instead of Amazon.com

    href
  • Look for generic greetings like "Dear Customer" instead of your name.
  • Be cautious of urgent or threatening language that pressures you to act quickly.
  • Verify links by hovering over them to see the actual URL before clicking.
  • Check the sender's email address or phone number for authenticity.
